A Temple We Are Building Together

We are building a temple
& a gau shala.

A modern gau shala for 21 cows, a hall for puja and archana, a garden, and rooms for weary travellers. Built by many small acts of seva.

Land identified on the outskirts of Ahmedabad, Gujarat
ॐ

Where there is a cow, a lamp, and a roof for the weary, a home becomes a temple.

For years we have helped devotees reach temples that already stand. Now we want to help raise one from bare ground, a place that gives before it ever asks. Twenty one cows will live here, cared for in a clean, modern shed with proper ventilation, fresh water on tap, year round fodder and regular veterinary attention.

Around them, a simple hall will hold the sound of aarti at dawn and dusk. A small garden will grow tulsi and flowers for the deity. And a handful of well kept rooms will let a pilgrim or a travelling family rest the night in comfort.

This is not a grand project. It is a small, honest one, and that is exactly why every rupee shows.

With folded hands, the Devanshya family
